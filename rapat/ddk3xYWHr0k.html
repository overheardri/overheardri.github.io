So, RUU Masyarakat Adat has been sitting on DPR's list for about 18 years. One day before reses, Baleg heard from PPMAN, lawyers who defend indigenous communities pro bono.

PPMAN handled about 229 cases in five years. The pattern? Warga adat defend land their families have used for generations, tapi karena wilayahnya belum diakui jelas, they end up facing criminal charges. Sometimes their lawyers too.

Their key ask: anti-SLAPP rules, so legal processes can't be used as revenge against people fighting for their rights peacefully. And they were clear: this is not asking to be above the law.

Anggota Baleg from across kubu partai (fraksi) were on board. One suggested writing down adat history before it disappears, and others debated whether recognition should come through perda or a faster SK bupati.
