Glossary

Terms you'll hear in DPR meetings

Indonesian parliament talk is full of acronyms. We keep the Indonesian term, because that's what you'll hear in the video, and explain it in plain English.

DPR RI
Indonesia's House of Representatives, elected in general elections. It makes laws, approves the state budget (APBN) and oversees the government.
Senayan
A common nickname for the DPR, because the parliament building is in the Senayan area of Jakarta.
Rapat Paripurna (plenary)
A meeting of all DPR members. This is where the DPR's official decisions are made, such as passing laws or approving officials.
Komisi (commission)
A DPR committee responsible for one policy area. For example, Commission III covers law and Commission VIII covers religion and social affairs, including hajj. Each commission works with matching government ministries.
Badan Legislasi (Baleg)
The DPR's legislation body, which coordinates the drafting of laws and harmonises bills.
Badan Anggaran (Banggar)
The DPR's budget body, which negotiates the state budget with the government.
BAKN
The State Financial Accountability Body: reviews audit findings from the Supreme Audit Agency (BPK) and how state money is managed.
BKSAP
The Inter-Parliamentary Cooperation Body: handles the DPR's relations with other countries' parliaments.
Fraksi (faction)
The group of DPR members from the same political party.
Raker (working meeting)
A commission meeting with a minister or the head of a ministry-level agency.
RDP (hearing)
A commission meeting with government officials below minister level, such as directors-general or agency heads.
RDPU (public hearing)
A meeting to hear from the public: experts, academics, organisations, students. One of the ways citizens help shape laws.
Panja (working committee)
A small team from a commission or body that examines one issue in detail, such as a bill's articles or hajj costs.
RUU (bill)
A draft law under discussion that is not yet in force.
Tingkat I and II deliberation
The two stages for a bill. Stage I is the detailed discussion in a commission or body with the government. Stage II is the decision in the plenary.
Usul inisiatif DPR (DPR-initiated bill)
A bill proposed by the DPR itself rather than by the government.
Masa Sidang (sitting period)
A period when the DPR sits in Jakarta. Each session year is split into several sitting periods.
Reses (recess)
The period when DPR members don't sit in Jakarta and go back to their electoral districts to hear from voters.
Uji kelayakan (fit-and-proper test)
The DPR questions candidates for public office before approving or choosing them.
PAW (interim replacement)
Replacing an official or member before their term ends.
BPIH
Hajj Operation Cost: the total cost of hajj per pilgrim, paid partly by the pilgrim and partly from the investment returns on hajj funds.
Perampasan aset (asset forfeiture)
The state taking over property that came from crime. The Asset Forfeiture Bill sets out how, including forfeiture without a criminal conviction (NCB).
Illicit enrichment
An official's wealth growing in a way they can't explain. Whether to make this a crime is still being debated.
Skors (adjournment)
Pausing a meeting, then resuming it later.
