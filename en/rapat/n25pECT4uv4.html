Terms in this meeting

LPP RRI Supervisory Board (Dewan Pengawas LPP RRI)
The body that oversees Radio Republik Indonesia as a public broadcasting institution. Its members are chosen by the DPR from candidates proposed by the President.
Fit-and-proper test (Uji kelayakan)
The DPR tests candidates for public office before choosing or approving them.
Interim replacement (Pengganti antarwaktu, PAW)
A reserve candidate who takes the place of a member who leaves before the term ends.
Recess (Reses)
The period when DPR members do not hold sittings and instead return to their home districts to hear constituents' concerns.
Level I deliberation (Pembicaraan tingkat I)
The stage of detailed deliberation of a bill in a commission or joint body with the government, before it is decided in the plenary session.
