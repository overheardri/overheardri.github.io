The 8th Plenary Session (Rapat Paripurna) of Sitting Period I of the 2026-2027 Session Year was held on Thursday, 1 October 2026, with 383 members attending, according to the attendance list from the Secretariat General. The session approved the report by Commission III on the fit and proper test of the candidate for National Police Chief (Kapolri): General Listyo Sigit Prabowo was honorably discharged, and Police Commissioner General Suyudi Ario Seto was approved as Kapolri. Commission III stated that approval at the commission level was reached by acclamation (aklamasi, agreement without a formal vote) by all factions. The session also designated the Java North Coast Management Authority (Badan Otorita Pengelola Pantai Utara Jawa) as a working partner of Commission IV, and approved an extension of the deadline for deliberating the Industrial Design Bill.
