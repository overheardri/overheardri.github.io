The House of Representatives' (DPR) Legislation Body (Baleg) held a working meeting on first-level deliberation (pembicaraan tingkat I) of the Copyright Bill with the government on 6 October 2026. Baleg Deputy Chair Martin Manurung explained the bill, which is a DPR initiative. It regulates the economic rights of broadcasters and press companies, works created with artificial intelligence, fair use, and licensing, royalties, and collective management organizations. The bill also follows up on Constitutional Court Decision No. 84/PUU-XXI/2023 so that the definition of a trade center covers the digital realm. The government, represented by the Minister of Law, submitted 1,117 items in its problem inventory list (DIM, Daftar Inventarisasi Masalah). The meeting agreed on a deliberation schedule with a target of finishing no later than 17 December 2026, a deliberation mechanism, and the formation of a working committee (Panja) chaired by Martin Manurung.

Key points

  1. 0:41The meeting was attended by 17 members from 8 factions (fraksi); a quorum was declared met and the meeting was open to the public.
  2. 10:09Martin Manurung explained that the Copyright Bill is a DPR initiative bill that was established at a plenary session on 12 March 2026.
  3. 11:15The bill follows up on Constitutional Court Decision No. 84/PUU-XXI/2023, which extends the definition of a trade center to the digital realm.
  4. 11:49The bill's aims: to adapt to the digital era, protect journalistic works, regulate the obligations of digital platforms, and protect traditional cultural expressions and AI-based works.
  5. 12:48The scope covers the economic rights of broadcasters and the press, AI-based works, fair use, duration of protection, licensing and royalties, disputes, and criminal provisions.
  6. 19:14The government proposed discussing the institutions of LMK (collective management organizations) and KMKN (the National Collective Management Commission), a royalty endowment fund, the legal status of AI works, and the protection of journalistic works.
  7. 20:13The government submitted 1,117 DIM: 558 unchanged, 91 amended, 143 proposed for deletion, 181 new substance, and 144 editorial.
  8. 23:29A member asked the government not to change substance through DIM labeled as repositioning or editorial, citing the deliberation of the One Data Indonesia Bill.
  9. 27:27The government asked that the Panja meeting not start on 7 October because the Ministry of Law team was hosting a WIPO (World Intellectual Property Organization) session in Bali.
  10. 29:11Baleg leadership stressed that Baleg does not hold closed drafting or deliberation meetings and asked the government to open up public participation.

Decisions and agreements

  • 30:43The deliberation mechanism was approved: DIM marked as unchanged are approved directly, while the other DIM are discussed in the Panja.
  • 31:41The Copyright Bill Panja was formed with Martin Manurung as chair.
