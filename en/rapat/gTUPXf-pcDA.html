A working group (Panja) of House Commission VIII of the DPR and the government's own Panja read out and agreed on the conclusions on the Hajj pilgrimage cost (BPIH) for 1448 H/2027 CE. Indonesia's Hajj quota was set at 221,000 people: 92% regular Hajj and 8% special Hajj (haji khusus). The meeting chair said that, after communicating with President Prabowo Subianto, the government will cover Rp3 million of the domestic cost component for each pilgrim. With that subsidy, the average final payment made by pilgrims is reported at around Rp23.2 million, about Rp3 million lower than in 2026. All factions (fraksi) and the government said they agreed. One member asked where the funding for the subsidy would come from.

Terms in this meeting

BPIH
Biaya Penyelenggaraan Ibadah Haji (the Hajj pilgrimage cost): the total Hajj cost per pilgrim. Part is paid by the pilgrim, and part comes from the benefit value of Hajj funds.
Benefit value (nilai manfaat)
The returns earned from BPKH's management of pilgrims' Hajj deposits, used to help cover Hajj costs.
BPKH
Badan Pengelola Keuangan Haji (the Hajj Financial Management Agency): the body that manages prospective pilgrims' deposited funds.
Initial deposit (setoran awal)
The money a prospective pilgrim pays when registering for Hajj to get a place in the queue.
Panja
Panitia kerja (working group): a small team within a commission that examines one issue in detail, here the Hajj cost.
