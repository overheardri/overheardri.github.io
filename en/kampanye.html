Presidential election 2024

Who stood on whose stage?

Three tickets, one election, and a lot of familiar faces from your TV. Here's who did what in 2024, with the news report for every line.

Hey, Senayan-ers. Your favorite screen stars had a busy campaign season. Spotted: MCs, declarations and front-row seats, for all three tickets. Receipts included.

A record of actors and actresses who publicly backed one of the three tickets in 2024: what they did, when, and the news report it comes from.

Backing a candidate is normal and lawful. This is a record, not a judgment, and all three tickets get the same treatment.

How a name gets on this page

  • Specific actA news report of something they did: a campaign-team role, hosting or performing at a campaign event, or a public declaration. Appearing only in a "celebrity supporters" roundup is not enough.
  • ActorsPeople known for acting, including comedians and presenters who act. Singers, bands and influencers are not covered here.
  • Public onlyOnly what the news reported. No personal details, no social media accounts.
  • Weaker evidence"Scheduled or attended" means named in a lineup or seen at a rally, without a reported statement of support.
  • Why fewer under 01Fewer actors campaigned on record for ticket 01; most of its celebrity support came from singers, comedians and influencers. Know a reported act we missed, or spot a mistake? Send us the news link. Report a mistake
