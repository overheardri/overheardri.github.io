Actors on the campaign trail

Who stood on which stage in 2024: actors and actresses who publicly backed one of the three tickets, with what they did, when, and the news report it comes from.

Supporting a candidate is a normal, lawful thing to do. This page is a record, not a judgment, and it treats all three tickets the same way.

How a name gets on this list

  • Specific actA news report of something they did: a campaign-team role, hosting or performing at a campaign event, or a public declaration. Being on a "celebrity supporters" roundup alone is not enough.
  • ActorsPeople known for acting, including comedians and presenters who act. Singers, bands and influencers are not covered here.
  • Public onlyOnly what was reported in the news. No personal details, and no social media accounts.
  • Weaker evidenceMarked "scheduled" or "attended": named in a lineup, or seen at a rally, without a reported statement.
