Who said what

Each profile collects what one DPR member said in open meetings. Every line links to the second in the video, so you can hear the original yourself.

Ever wondered what the people you voted for actually say in meetings? Here's the record, meeting by meeting, with the second of video.

Who said what, and when? We kept the receipts, meeting by meeting, down to the second.

Early sample: 5 members of Commission III, each from 10 or more meetings. Speakers are identified from the chair inviting them to speak, then checked by hand. Summaries are AI-written and not yet checked by a human.

10 meetings
Hinca Panjaitan
Partai Demokrat · Commission III

Talks most about: Police, Asset forfeiture, Land and agrarian issues.

Bang Hinca from North Sumatra (Sumut) is a regular with questions on the Asset Forfeiture Bill (RUU Perampasan Aset) and the national police (Polri). His trademark: wordplay, like "merampas, kalau enggak pas tinggal ampas" [seize it, and if it's not done right, you're left with the dregs] 😄

Hey, Senayan-ers. Meet Hinca Panjaitan, Demokrat, from the North Sumatra III electoral district (dapil). His thing? Asset forfeiture, the police, and wordplay that literally goes viral.
10 meetings
Sarifuddin Sudding
Partai Amanat Nasional · Commission III

Talks most about: Land and agrarian issues, Asset forfeiture, Judiciary.

Pak Sudding is a PAN member from Luwu and a former lawyer. His go-to topics: customary land (tanah adat), the land mafia, and putting brakes on the powers of officials.

Hey, Senayan-ers. Sarifuddin Sudding, PAN, from Luwu, ex-lawyer. His recurring storyline? Customary land vs. big companies, and keeping officials in check.
16 meetings
Safaruddin
PDI Perjuangan · Commission III

Talks most about: Judiciary, Asset forfeiture, Budget.

General Safaruddin is a retired police officer, a former provincial police chief (Kapolda), now with PDI Perjuangan. His style is straight to the point and he likes direct dialogue, especially with the police.

Hey, Senayan-ers. Safaruddin, PDI Perjuangan, a retired police general. His focus? The police, the courts, and the new Criminal Code (KUHP), asked straight to the officers' faces.
13 meetings
Bimantoro Wiyono
Partai Gerindra · Commission III

Talks most about: Judiciary, Asset forfeiture, Narcotics.

Mas Bimantoro is from Gerindra, the child of a court clerk who later became a judge. He often gives appreciation, but also asks sharp questions about whose interests lie behind a bill.

Hey, Senayan-ers. Bimantoro Wiyono, Gerindra, raised in a courthouse family. He often opens with appreciation, then asks the sharp questions.
12 meetings
I Wayan Sudirta
PDI Perjuangan · Commission III

Talks most about: Asset forfeiture, Judiciary, Police.

Pak Wayan is from Bali, PDI Perjuangan, a former legal aid (LBH) activist. His questions go down to the article level, and he often says "saya dukung, tapi..." [I support it, but...].

Hey, Senayan-ers. I Wayan Sudirta, PDI Perjuangan, Bali electoral district (dapil), ex-legal aid (LBH). His style? Supportive, with article-by-article notes.
