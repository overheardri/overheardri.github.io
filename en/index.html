Their message? The limits have to be clear, so the law doesn't wander off and hit ordinary citizens ⚖️Asset Forfeiture Bill: expert and two bar associations ask for due process safeguards, with a 15 December target for passage

This bill determines when the state may take assets suspected of coming from crime, including without a criminal verdict. The rules touch the property rights of every citizen: who has to prove what, how third parties who buy goods in good faith are protected, and who is responsible if seized assets are damaged or lost.
