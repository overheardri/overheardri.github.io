  1. 27 October 2026 Asset forfeiture 1:05:36

    Said that proceeds from asset forfeiture would become non-tax state revenue (PNBP), cautioned that the method must not run against the law and human rights and that asset values must not collapse ("merampas, kalau enggak pas tinggal ampas" [seizing it badly leaves only dregs]), then asked about the 13 types of crime planned as targets.

    Reminded everyone: seized assets must go into the state treasury without breaking the law or human rights, and their value shouldn't collapse. Then asked about the 13 types of crime the bill would target.

    Spotted: dropping the line "merampas, kalau enggak pas tinggal ampas" [seize it, and if it's not done right, you're left with the dregs]. Basically, seizing assets is fine, but don't let their value turn into dregs.

    Public hearing (RDPU) on the Asset Forfeiture Bill with experts and academics · Read the meeting summary
  2. 7 October 2026 Asset forfeiture 1:43:38

    After an expert cited failures in Belgium, Colombia, and the United Kingdom, asked for a study of which countries have run asset forfeiture successfully, and asked for a formula for the rights and access to defend themselves for people whose assets are seized.

    The expert witnesses described how many countries have failed at this. Bang Hinca asked right back: is there any country that succeeded? He also wants people whose assets are seized to still have the right to defend themselves.

    Everyone talked about failures abroad. He asked the real question: who actually got it right? Plus, a fair trial for those whose assets are seized.

    Public hearing (RDPU) on the Asset Forfeiture Bill with experts and academics
  3. 1 September 2026 Police 48:02

    Proposed studying security for national vital objects as a source of non-tax revenue (PNBP) for the police, which by their calculation could reach Rp7 trillion, asked for attention to police personnel in border areas, then said the Demokrat faction approves the 2027 police budget.

    His proposal: guarding vital national sites (objek vital nasional) could become a source of income for Polri, up to Rp7 trillion, he says. He also asked for attention to police officers at the borders. In the end, Demokrat approved Polri's 2027 budget.

    The pitch: vital national sites (objek vital nasional) could be a cash cow for Polri, up to Rp7 trillion by his math (check video). And Demokrat said yes to Polri's 2027 budget.

    Working meeting (Raker) with the National Police Chief (Kapolri) and the head of the financial intelligence unit (PPATK)
  4. 26 August 2026 Land and agrarian issues 2:07:46

    Read out the meeting's conclusions, including a request that the Criminal Investigation Agency (Bareskrim) and regional police (Polda) follow up on reports of 147 alleged criminalization cases against members of the Agrarian Reform Consortium in 11 provinces.

    He was the one who read out the conclusions: the police criminal investigation agency (Bareskrim) is asked to follow up on 147 reports of alleged criminalization of agrarian activists in 11 provinces.

    On mic duty: reading out the meeting's conclusions, including 147 reports of alleged criminalization of agrarian activists. That's a lot of cases.

  5. 4 August 2026 Asset forfeiture 47:47

    Said the deliberations were deliberately made long so the substance is right, then asked which institution should be given authority to manage assets from crime, for example a State Revenue Agency or an execution agency, citing data on execution requests at the Supreme Court.

    He said the bill is being discussed slowly on purpose, to get it right. The main question: who will hold the assets from crime? The State Revenue Agency? An execution agency?

    "Bukan mau melambat-lambatkan" [we're not trying to slow things down], he said. The big question: who gets to hold the seized assets? Power matters, bestie.

    Public hearing (RDPU) on the Asset Forfeiture Bill with Yeheskiel Minggus Tiranda and Firman Wijaya
  6. 5 June 2026 Police 1:24:04

    Proposed that security for national vital objects, currently governed by Presidential Decree 63 of 2004, be raised into the Police Law, citing the Plumpang depot explosion and the power outage in Sumatra.

    He proposes upgrading the protection of vital national sites (refineries, fuel depots, power) from a presidential decree (Keppres) to being written into the Polri Law. His examples: the Plumpang depot explosion and the Sumatra blackout.

    Remember Plumpang? Remember the Sumatra blackout? He wants vital national sites protected by law, not just a presidential decree (Keppres).

    Public hearing (RDPU) on the Police Bill with the Dean of UPI and academics from the USU Faculty of Law
  7. 3 June 2026 Police 57:38

    Asked whether, after the Constitutional Court (MK) ruling on which positions police officers may hold, the law enforcement cluster could cover customs and excise as well as witness and victim protection.

    After the Constitutional Court (MK) ruling on active police officers holding civilian posts, he asked: can police be placed in customs or witness protection?

    Post-MK ruling question of the day: can police officers be placed in customs or the witness and victim protection agency (LPSK)?

    Public hearing (RDPU) on the Police Bill with the Dean of the UI Faculty of Law, a Untirta professor, and Unila academics
  8. 13 May 2026 Judiciary 1:39:48
