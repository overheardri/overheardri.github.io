About

So anyone can read what their representatives say

Open meetings of Indonesia's parliament are broadcast every day, but they run for hours and the language is technical. Overheard RI helps people understand what was discussed, who said what and what was decided, with a direct link to the source video.

Independent

Overheard RI is a citizen project. We're not affiliated with the DPR, the government or any political party, and we don't take money from them.

Report a mistake

If you find a summary that's wrong, tell us the meeting title, the minute in the video, and what it should say. An email address for corrections will be posted here soon. Every correction is noted on the meeting's page.

Help out

We're looking for volunteer reviewers: people willing to watch parts of meetings and check the summaries against the video.
